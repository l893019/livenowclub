# Author Outreach Plan

## Overview
Reach out to authors, writers, and publications to share two essays that explore mortality, purpose, and meaning in the face of terminal illness and technological change.

## Target Essays

### 1. **Fixing the Unfixable**
https://livenowclub.com/read/expecting-the-unfixable

**Themes**:
- Game theory and Nash equilibrium in terminal illness
- What to say when nothing can be said
- The unfixable nature of mortality
- Conditional hope vs. unconditional presence

**Comparable works**:
- Tatiana Schlossberg's New Yorker piece on terminal leukemia
- Pema Chödrön on accepting what is
- Camus on "the plague"

### 2. **Soul** (When Purpose Is All That Is Left)
https://livenowclub.com/read/soul

**Themes**:
- Productivity vs. being
- Disability and worth beyond labor
- AI, automation, and the future of meaning
- Rewriting your personal operating system
- What remains when doing is removed

**Comparable works**:
- Neal Stephenson's The Diamond Age
- Heidegger's being-toward-death
- Science fiction as philosophy

## Notes

- These essays are VERY strong and literary - don't undersell them
- Lead with the personal (Stage IV cancer, permanent disability) - that's your credibility
- Be specific about what you admire in their work - show you've actually read them
- Don't ask for anything beyond reading/sharing - make it easy
- Follow up once after 2 weeks if no response, then move on
- Consider adapting "Soul" for Aeon (philosophy magazine) - they love sci-fi + philosophy
- Consider pitching "Fixing the Unfixable" to The New Yorker given the Tatiana Schlossberg reference
